🪿 The Goose Watch

“The Goose is observing the lot.”

How Clues Work: Visibly parked cars and concrete poles are already shown on the lot. Every unknown ? stall is either an Available Safe Space or a Hidden Hazard (Loose Cart, The Goose, Eternal Reverse Driver, or Mystery Stain). Each circular clue badge (0–8) equals the exact count of Hidden Hazards in the 8 surrounding stalls. Combine clues and flag (⚠️) hazards to verify safe spaces before parking!

How to Play Parking Lot

Goal: Find and commit to any safe, available parking space before you run out of patience. You do not need to clear the board!

  • The Strategic Dilemma: Every safe space has a quality rating from Excellent (1.65×, 5 stars) near the entrance down to Risky (0.55×, 1 star) in the distant corners. Do you take a Good or Great spot right now while your Time Efficiency is high, or spend more time and patience hunting for a front-row gem?
  • Minesweeper Clues: Every inspected stall displays the exact number (0–8) of adjacent Trouble Stalls (Loose Carts, The Goose, Eternal Reverse Lights, Mystery Stains, Blocked Poles, and Oversized Bad Parkers). Ordinary parked sedans are harmless and don't add to the trouble count.
  • Patience vs. Elapsed Time: Elapsed time counts up for your Time Efficiency Multiplier. Patience only drains when you inspect spaces or stumble directly into a frustrating hazard—never while you stop to think!
  • Accidents & Deductibles: Parking in a wide-open spot has a 0% accident risk. Parking next to loose carts, oversized trucks, or The Goose carries a clearly displayed risk of a collision penalty and insurance deductible.
